Chapter 1 Uploader

Chapter 1 is a YouTube channel of animated history: weekly documentaries about famous moments in history, and short comedy rap battles between famous people from the past.

Chapter 1 Uploader is the channel's own publishing tool. It uploads the videos the channel makes to the channel itself, with their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, subtitles and publishing times, using the YouTube Data API.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Chapter 1 channel. It is not offered to anyone else and does not act on any other YouTube account.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads Chapter 1's videos to the Chapter 1 channel.
  • Sets each video's details: title, description, tags, thumbnail, subtitles, language and scheduled publishing time.
  • Reads public statistics of the channel's own videos (views and likes) to see what viewers enjoy.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. How the tool handles data is set out in its privacy policy.
