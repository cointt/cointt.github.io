Privacy Policy

Chapter 1 Uploader. Last updated 11 October 2026.

Who this applies to

Chapter 1 Uploader is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Chapter 1 YouTube channel and Snapchat profile to publish Chapter 1's own videos. It has no other users and no sign-up.

YouTube API Services

Chapter 1 Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Snapchat

Chapter 1 Uploader uses Snap's Public Profile API to post Chapter 1's short videos to its own Snapchat Public Profile (Spotlight and Stories). Snap's handling of data is described in the Snap Terms of Service and the Snap Privacy Policy.

What information it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, given once through Google's sign-in and once through Snapchat's sign-in, the tool can:

  • upload videos to the Chapter 1 channel and set their title, description, tags, thumbnail, subtitles, language and publishing time;
  • read basic details and public statistics (such as views and likes) of the Chapter 1 channel's own videos;
  • upload short videos to the Chapter 1 Snapchat Public Profile and post them to Spotlight or Stories with a short description.

It does not access viewers' personal information, comments, messages, watch history, or any other YouTube or Snapchat account.

How the information is used

Only to publish Chapter 1's videos and to see how they perform. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How it is stored

The sign-in permissions (access tokens issued by Google and Snap) are kept as encrypted secrets in the channel owner's private GitHub repository and on the owner's own computer. Video statistics are kept in that private repository. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Sharing

No information is sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party.

Cookies and tracking

The tool has no website users, so it uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. This page itself sets no cookies.

Removing access

The channel owner can remove the tool's access at any time in their Google Account's security settings, and in Snapchat under Settings, Connected Apps. Stored statistics are deleted on request or when the tool is retired.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
